# ASHFALL VO cast (Revision 2: Truckee Meadows, Nevada)

Generated speech (Kokoro-82M, Apache-2.0, offline) shaped per character. Nothing here is flat TTS output: every line is built
phrase by phrase, each phrase with its own pitch and tempo, with designed pauses and synthesized breaths or coughs between them.
Then the character's voice color goes on (rasp, fry, whisper, vibrato, tremble, shiver). The act and the scene change it further,
and the medium goes on last (radio, ham, phone, loudspeaker, gas mask, drone vocoder, husk, Hollow).
Acts follow the Rev 2 seasons: `blackout` = Ch1-2 late-summer heat and dust (dry throat, a little rasp, **no cold shiver**),
`collapse` = Ch3-4 autumn, `civil_war` = Ch5-7 sleet and war (compression, strain, first shivers), `nuclear_winter` = Ch8-10
ash and sub-zero survival horror (shiver, hoarseness, breathiness, longer pauses).
Recipes live in `audio/tools/vo/cast.json` and `barks.json`. This file is generated by `tools/vo/make_cast_md.py`.

**How to read a recipe.** *Voice* is a weighted blend of Kokoro style vectors, which gives a unique timbre per character.
*Semitones* shift pitch and formants together, like tape, so it changes apparent age and size: kids +3, teens +1.5, elders -1.5 to -2.
*Speed* is the real talking speed, because the TTS tempo is pre-compensated for the pitch shift.
*Pauses* are seconds after a sentence, ellipsis or dash. A per-character jitter makes the rhythm human, and *breath odds* is how often a breath fills a pause.
Emotions are presets of deltas (in `cast.json → emotions`). Scenes and individual lines pick them.
Lines with `!` or ALL-CAPS words are rendered as shouts automatically (+1 st, +3 dB, drive).

## Signal chains (in `tools/vo/dsp.py`)

| chain | used for | recipe |
|---|---|---|
| `phone` | Ross's Day 0 voicemail (`ch01_voicemail`) | handset band 320 to 3300 Hz, +4 dB at 1.7 kHz, codec bite (soft clip), compression, faint line hiss |
| `hollow` | Hollow mimicry (`hollow` lines + `hollow` mimic barks) | the BORROWED character's own base voice (blend, pitch, rasp) with all life removed: no breaths, coughs, shiver, tremble or jitter; questions flattened; every repeated phrase is the identical take; wrong gaps (a rushed 50 ms join, then a 0.85 to 1.25 s hold) and a 160 ms hole mid-phrase; 7.2 ms feedback comb (metallic, empty-chest resonance), a dark 19 ms doubled shadow, +3.5 dB at 420 Hz / -4 dB at 3 kHz, a hard gate so nothing breathes between words, cold outdoor tail |
| `radio` | Carly/Augie/Roz `[On the radio]`, Kit's last call, Ironwood/Guard chatter barks | HP 350 / band 420 to 2800 Hz, +5 dB at 1.6 kHz, 8:1 compression, soft clipping, static bed while keyed, squelch click on key-up and a 'kssht' tail |
| `ham` | Augie on air (KK7AUG), the Night Owl | SSB-ish band 300 to 2500 Hz, slow QSB fading, faint heterodyne whistle, crackle and static, lead-in and tail |
| `loudspeaker` | Kessler `[Loudspeaker]` | horn band 500 to 4200 Hz, +7 dB at 2.1 kHz, overdriven driver, slapback echoes at 170, 410 and 830 ms off buildings, cold-air reverb tail |
| `drone` | Ironwood drone PA barks | 18-band channel vocoder on a 92 Hz saw+square monotone carrier with noise, mixed with a band-passed ghost of the dry voice, ~8 kHz sample-and-hold and coarse quantize, then the loudspeaker chain |
| `mask` | Ironwood Remnant (Act III) | LP 2.9 kHz, rubber-cavity peak +9 dB at 1.15 kHz, notch at 2.6 kHz, 1.1 ms canister comb, compression, synthesized inhale through the filter and an exhale with valve-flap clicks around every line |
| `husk` | the Ch8 husk (Pete Lund) + husk barks | the same human voice, but pitch can't hold (irregular 4 Hz vibrato), shredded rasp with fry, wet chest gurgle blips gated by the voice, breath leaking through every word (whisper layer 38%), unsteady tremolo. Phrases are spoken at uneven tempos with wheezing gaps. Sick and broken, never monstrous |
| (none) | everyone in person | voice color only; 3D placement and room come from the game at runtime. `playLine(..., {radio:true})` adds a live walkie-talkie chain for any line |
